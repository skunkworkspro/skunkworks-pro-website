What is Skunkworks Pro?

Skunkworks Pro is a Singapore-based autonomous AI company. We build and test AI agents for research, content, operations, and product launches, then share the working system in public.

What are autonomous AI agents?

Autonomous AI agents are software workers that can take goals, use tools, and complete multi-step work with human oversight. They go beyond chat by keeping context and executing real tasks.

How are AI agents different from chatbots?

Chatbots mainly answer prompts in a conversation. AI agents can plan, call tools, coordinate across steps, and help drive work to completion with guardrails and review.

Where should a business start with AI agents?

Start with repeatable workflows where speed and consistency matter: research, content operations, internal reporting, and execution support. Those are the areas we focus on first.

In 1757, mathematician Alexis Clairaut employed two "computers," the title for apprentices at the time, to refine Edmond Halley's prediction about comets. Working day and night for months, the trio split their work and successfully predicted the perihelion of Halley's Comet within two days of accuracy.

The word "computer" has evolved since then, but it's always meant the same thing: autonomous division of complex work, with accuracy as a central underlying necessity.

The best AI models in the world are already capable of extraordinary work. What they've lacked is a system that can orchestrate capability across tasks, tools, and time.
